# Sheet Tabs

Shows or hides the worksheet tabs at the bottom of the Excel window with one click.

## How to use

Click **Sheet Tabs** to hide the worksheet tabs in the active workbook window. Click it again to show them. For example, hide the tabs while presenting a workbook and restore them when you need to navigate between sheets.

This changes the active window's tab display; it does not delete or hide worksheets themselves.

The add-in is free and open source. [Installation instructions](/en/install/).
